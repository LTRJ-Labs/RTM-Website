Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a quote, or register interest in MothNode, we collect information that you voluntarily provide to us. This includes:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name (first name, last name).
  • Contact Information: Email address.
  • Professional Details: Organization name (e.g., fire district or public works department).
  • Request Context: Details about your installation needs, dry hydrant configurations, and cistern deployment goals.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to fulfill your requests and provide the best service possible, specifically to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests for system quotes.
  • Assess cistern or hydrant site installation needs to prepare custom configurations.
  • Communicate updates, alerts, and field deployment details relating to MothNode.
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our communication systems.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard physical, electronic, and administrative security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction. We do not sell or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Your Rights and Contact

You have the right to request access to the information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask for the deletion of your personal data. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please reach out to us at privacy@mothnode.com.
