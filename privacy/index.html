Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

1. The short version

This website does not collect anything about you. There are no forms to fill in, no accounts to create, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Nothing you do here is recorded by us.

2. What our host records

This site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers log routine technical information when a page is requested, which can include your IP address, browser type, and the page you asked for. GitHub uses those logs to keep the service running and to prevent abuse. We do not receive, store, or have access to them. GitHub's handling of that data is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Links to other sites

This site links out to LTRJ Labs and to our LinkedIn page. Once you follow a link, you are on someone else's site and their privacy practices apply, not ours.

4. If you email us

If you contact us directly, we receive whatever you choose to put in that message, such as your name, email address, and the department you work for. We use it only to reply to you and to follow up about MothNode. We do not sell it, trade it, or add you to a mailing list without your say-so. Ask us to delete your message and we will.

5. Monitoring units and the dashboard

This policy covers this website only. MothNode monitoring hardware and the MothNode Dashboard record tank and device readings, and how that information is handled is set out separately in the terms provided with a deployment.

6. Changes and contact

If this site ever starts collecting information, for example by adding a contact form or a mailing list, this policy will be updated before that goes live. Questions about it can go to privacy@mothnode.com.
